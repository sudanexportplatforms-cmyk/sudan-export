Sudan Export - Full Stack B2B Export Platform

Build a production-ready full-stack web application called “Sudan Export”.

Business Overview

Sudan Export is a B2B export marketplace connecting Sudanese exporters and suppliers with international buyers.

The first release should focus on:

* Buyer RFQ Management
* Supplier Management
* Supplier Quotations
* Admin Approval Workflow
* Product Catalog
* Company Verification
* Email Notifications
* Dashboard Reporting

This is NOT an e-commerce store.

The core workflow is:

Buyer → RFQ → Suppliers → Quotations → Comparison → Selection → Deal
